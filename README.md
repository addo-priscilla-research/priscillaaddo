## 🌦️ About Me

I am an **atmospheric and climate researcher** with a strong interest in understanding the physical processes that drive weather and climate variability, extreme weather, and environmental change.

My research interests span **mesoscale meteorology, severe weather, extreme precipitation, atmospheric dynamics, climate variability, air quality, land–atmosphere interactions, and hydrometeorology**.

I use **numerical modeling, satellite remote sensing, reanalysis datasets, GIS, and scientific programming** to investigate atmospheric and environmental processes across different spatial and temporal scales.

My technical experience includes **WRF, WRF-Post, ERA5, GPM/IMERG, Google Earth Engine, Python, Fortran, MATLAB, ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, and machine-learning methods** for atmospheric and environmental data analysis.

My research experience includes investigating **African Easterly Waves and extreme precipitation over West Africa, Penman–Monteith evapotranspiration, urban heat island dynamics, air-quality variability, and land-surface and atmospheric processes**.

I am particularly interested in combining **high-resolution modeling, observations, and data-driven approaches** to better understand and predict high-impact weather and climate-related hazards.

I am passionate about developing **reproducible, data-driven research workflows** and translating atmospheric and climate research into knowledge that can support improved weather prediction, environmental monitoring, climate resilience, and sustainable decision-making.

## 📊 Research Projects

### 🌧️ African Easterly Waves & Extreme Precipitation over West Africa

Investigation of the relationship between **African Easterly Waves, atmospheric dynamics, and extreme precipitation** over West Africa using atmospheric and precipitation datasets.

**Focus:** Atmospheric dynamics • Tropical meteorology • Extreme precipitation • West African climate

**Tools:** `Python` `ERA5` `GPM/IMERG` `Atmospheric Diagnostics`

---

### 🌡️ Urban Heat Island Dynamics in Ghana

Satellite-based assessment of **land-surface temperature, land-use/land-cover change, and urban climate variability** to investigate urban heat island dynamics.

**Focus:** Urban climate • Land-surface temperature • Remote sensing • Land-use change

**Tools:** `Google Earth Engine` `Landsat` `MODIS` `GIS` `Python`

---

### 💧 Penman–Monteith Evapotranspiration Modeling

Assessment of how **measured and estimated net radiation and soil heat flux** influence Penman–Monteith evapotranspiration estimates.

**Focus:** Surface energy balance • Evapotranspiration • Land–atmosphere interactions • Hydrometeorology

**Tools:** `Python` `Meteorological Data` `Statistical Analysis`

---

### 🌫️ NO₂ Trends & Meteorological Controls in Kumasi, Ghana

Analysis of satellite-derived **Sentinel-5P/TROPOMI NO₂ observations** and meteorological conditions to investigate atmospheric pollution patterns, temporal trends, and meteorological controls.

**Focus:** Air quality • Atmospheric pollution • Meteorological variability • Remote sensing

**Tools:** `Python` `Google Earth Engine` `Sentinel-5P/TROPOMI` `NASA POWER` `Pandas` `Matplotlib`
