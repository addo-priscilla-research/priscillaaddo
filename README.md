# 👩🏾‍🔬 About Me

I am an **Atmospheric Science and Climate researcher** with research interests spanning **atmospheric wave dynamics, severe and hazardous weather, extreme-weather prediction, tropical cyclone dynamics, convective storms, climate variability and change, numerical weather prediction, and machine learning applications in meteorology**.

My research combines **high-resolution numerical modeling, reanalysis datasets, satellite observations, atmospheric diagnostics, statistical analysis, and machine learning** to investigate the physical processes governing high-impact weather and climate events.

I am particularly interested in understanding how **tropical atmospheric disturbances, moisture transport, convection, land-surface conditions, and large-scale climate variability** interact to produce extreme precipitation and other hazardous weather phenomena.

## 🧪 Selected Projects

### 🌀 Tropical Cyclone Genesis & Rapid Intensification — Tropical Atlantic

Investigation of the **dynamical and thermodynamic controls of tropical-cyclone genesis and rapid intensification** using high-resolution WRF simulations, reanalysis, satellite observations, and storm-track data.

**Research Focus:**
`Tropical Cyclones` `Genesis & Rapid Intensification` `Atmospheric Dynamics` `Tropical Meteorology`

**Tools:**
`WRF` `ERA5` `GPM/IMERG` `NOAA SST` `IBTrACS` `Python` `NCL` `NetCDF`


### 🌧️ African Easterly Waves & Extreme Precipitation — West Africa

Investigation of the relationship between **African Easterly Waves, atmospheric dynamics, and extreme precipitation** over West Africa using reanalysis and satellite precipitation datasets.

**Research Focus:**  
`Tropical Meteorology` `Atmospheric Dynamics` `Extreme Precipitation` `West African Climate`

**Tools:**  
`Python` `ERA5` `GPM/IMERG` `xarray` `SciPy` `Matplotlib` `Cartopy`

---

### 🌡️ Urban Heat Island Dynamics in Ghana

Satellite-based assessment of **land-surface temperature, land-use/land-cover change, and urban climate variability** to investigate urban heat island dynamics in Ghanaian cities.

**Research Focus:**  
`Urban Climate` `Land-Surface Temperature` `Remote Sensing` `LULC Change`

**Tools:**  
`Google Earth Engine` `Landsat` `MODIS` `GIS` `Python`

---

### 💧 Penman–Monteith Evapotranspiration Modeling

Assessment of how **measured and estimated net radiation and soil heat flux** influence Penman–Monteith evapotranspiration estimates.

**Research Focus:**  
`Surface Energy Balance` `Evapotranspiration` `Land–Atmosphere Interactions` `Hydrometeorology`

**Tools:**  
`Python` `Meteorological Data` `Statistical Analysis`

---

### 🌫️ NO₂ Trends & Meteorological Controls — Kumasi, Ghana

Analysis of satellite-derived **Sentinel-5P/TROPOMI NO₂ observations** and meteorological conditions to investigate temporal trends, seasonal variability, and meteorological controls on atmospheric pollution.

**Research Focus:**  
`Air Quality` `Atmospheric Pollution` `Remote Sensing` `Meteorological Variability`

**Tools:**  
`Python` `Google Earth Engine` `Sentinel-5P/TROPOMI` `NASA POWER` `Pandas` `Matplotlib`
